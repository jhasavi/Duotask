## LinkedIn (Tue)
> I kept having the same conversation at home: "Did anyone book that?" "I thought you had it."
>
> So I built a small app to end it. DuoTask is a shared to-do list for two people. Tasks start unclaimed, one of you takes it, and both of you can see who owns what. No nagging, no scrolling back through a chat thread.
>
> The part I found most interesting to build: you can send a single task to someone as a link over WhatsApp or text, and they can reply without making an account. Getting the second person to sign up is where most shared-list apps fall down.
>
> It's free and runs in the browser. I'd value feedback from anyone who shares a home or co-parents: what would make you actually use it? [link]
