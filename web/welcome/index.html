Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Sharing tasks, claiming, nudges and recurring chores are free. Optional paid extras may come later, and one subscription would cover both of you.

Does my partner need to install anything?

No. DuoTask runs in the browser. You can add it to your home screen from your browser's share menu so it opens like an app.

How do I put it on my home screen?

iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". It then opens full screen like an app.

Who can see my tasks?

Only you and the person you pair with. Personal tasks stay private to you. See the privacy policy in the app's Settings.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, from Settings. That erases your account and tasks.
